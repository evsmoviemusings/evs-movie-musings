Evil Dead Rise

Every Evil Dead film has to answer the same question: can it still make me flinch after all these years? This one finds a genuinely nasty new answer, trading the cabin in the woods for a cramped apartment and somehow making the walls feel just as merciless. It's mean in the ways this series has always earned the right to be.
